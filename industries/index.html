Questions, Answered

See all FAQs

Lenders and funders look at time in business, monthly deposits, industry, existing financing and credit history, which is one factor among several. We match your file with the ones whose criteria fit. Typical minimums are 1+ year in business and $20,000+ in monthly deposits; meeting them doesn't guarantee approval. Not every business qualifies.

Same-day business funding is fast working capital for businesses with steady revenue. We shop your request across a wide range of funders and walk you through their offers. Funders typically structure it as a purchase of a portion of your future receivables rather than a loan: the funder pays you upfront and receives an agreed percentage of your receivables as you collect them, without charging interest. Funding is subject to approval and timing varies: funds can arrive as soon as same day after approval and signing.

Usually your last 4 months of business bank statements, a government ID and a voided business check. Your advisor will tell you if anything else is needed.
